A selection of the projects I lead in biomedical sensing systems at the University of Stavanger: sensing infrastructures, datasets, my research lab, and software tools for the research community. Each card opens the project's own page.

Research ProjectSOSC

Smart Objects for Smart Care

A sensing infrastructure that instruments everyday objects with smart tags and combines them with smartwatch data, capturing how people interact with objects of daily living to infer activity labels without manual annotation. Deployed at Helse Campus Stavanger.

NFR Commercialization · Project 347595 · PI

DatasetFLAIR

Free-Living Activities for Independent-living Recognition

A wrist-worn inertial dataset of daily activities recorded in self-paced, unscripted, free-living conditions, built to support clinically grounded activity recognition for elderly home care that works in real homes, not just in the lab.

Human Activity Recognition · Ambient Assisted Living

Research LabMMAI Lab

Multimodal AI Lab

Sensing. Understanding. Improving Lives. The lab combines motion and biomechanics, physiological and neural sensing, vision and contactless sensing with AI and edge computing for health and well-being.

Software ToolPCC

Program Chair Copilot

A free desktop tool that automates the repetitive work of running a conference program: PDF compliance checks, conflict-of-interest detection, review analysis and program building. It runs locally, and AI features use your own chosen LLM provider.
